# Alert Center

A concise, public-facing overview of Alert Center.

## What is Alert Center?
Alert Center is a managed package for Salesforce that helps teams deliver timely, targeted notifications across email and in-app channels, with governance and auditability.

## Who is it for?
- RevOps and Sales teams that need consistent, policy-aligned alerts
- Customer success and support teams who rely on timely triggers
- Admins who value configuration-first extensibility

## What’s included here
This site hosts only non-technical materials: positioning, roadmap highlights, and high-level capabilities. Engineering specs and source code remain private.
