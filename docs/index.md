## Why Alert Center?

The cost of a missed update is staggering. A delayed notification means:
- Lost revenue on deal progression  
- Support tickets escalating unnecessarily  
- Customer success teams flying blind  
- Compliance gaps in critical workflows  

Most teams cobble together email templates, Slack integrations, and custom code. It's fragile. It's unmaintainable. **It's yesterday's approach.**

Alert Center is the governed, auditable notification engine purpose-built for Salesforce. Deploy in hours. Scale across your org. Never miss an update again.
